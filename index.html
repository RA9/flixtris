Tips to improve

01

Keep your stack low and flat to have more time to react to new pieces.

02

Build with a well on one side (usually right) to set up Tetrises with the I-piece.

03

Use the Hold feature to save I-pieces for Tetris opportunities.

04

Practice looking ahead at the Next piece to plan placements faster.

05

Avoid creating holes under pieces—they're hard to clear and waste lines.

How grading works

APM · Actions per minute
How quickly you move and rotate pieces. Higher APM shows faster decision-making and execution.
LPM · Lines per minute
How efficiently you clear lines. Skilled players keep LPM high by consistently setting up clears.
Tetris rate
The share of line clears that are Tetrises (4 lines at once). Tetrises score far more points.
Board control
Based on average stack height and holes created. Lower stacks and fewer holes mean better control.
